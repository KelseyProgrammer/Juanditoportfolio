# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Art directors and brand producers hiring a photographer for editorial, campaign, and lookbook shoots. They typically evaluate the portfolio on their phone, between other candidates, and need to judge the work and reach a booking contact within seconds.

## Product Purpose

A single-page portfolio that gets Juandito booked. Success is a hire inquiry: an art director scans the work, trusts the range, and emails or DMs.

## Positioning

Miami-based light with travel range: a photographer rooted in Miami's warm, sun-driven color who shoots on location wherever the job goes. (Confirmed by the user as the site's differentiator.)

## Operating Context

- Evaluated fast and mostly on mobile; the archive must carry the argument without explanation.
- The site is a Next.js 15 app in the GitHub repo (KelseyProgrammer/Juanditoportfolio); photos live in `public/photos/` with the manifest in `lib/photos.ts`.
- **Hosting:** live at https://juanditobandito.netlify.app on Netlify, connected to the GitHub repo — every push to `main` auto-deploys.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 169-frame photo archive across named shoots (portrait, fashion campaign, swim/lifestyle, travel journal), web-optimized at 1800px.
- The Darkroom offers a contact-sheet mode — a dense grid with the selects circled — built for art directors who scan archives that way.
- Booking channels: Juanherrep@yahoo.com and Instagram @juanditobandito.
- Hero tagline ("Real light, warm color, nothing over-produced.") and the About bio were drafted by the agent from the archive and confirmed positioning — no invented biography (no years-active or origin claims). The user may replace them with personal specifics.
- **Undecided / pending from the user:** the darkroom "Journal · <date>" captions, which are derived from file dates rather than confirmed shoot names.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Juandito** (Instagram: @juanditobandito). Sign-off line: "Postmarked Miami, FL".
- Client and subject names in the archive are cleared for public captions: Bradley Theodore, SS23 Heritage Sport, Watababe Editorial, and model first names (Zan, Maile, Annie, Gracie, Joy, Nikki, …). Confirmed by the user 2026-09-25.

## Evidence on Hand

- The real shoot archive itself (`public/photos/`), including recognizable commissioned work (Bradley Theodore portrait series, SS23 Heritage Sport campaign, Watababe Editorial).
- No testimonials, press quotes, or client logos are on hand — future work must not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. The photographs are the argument — every surface defers to the work; interface recedes.
2. Bookable in seconds on a phone: contact is always one scroll or tap away.
3. Real shoots, real names, and nothing invented — captions and claims trace to the archive.
4. Miami light, mobile range — location identity is product truth, not decoration.
5. Bracketed placeholders are ship-blockers: the site does not promote until email, tagline, and bio are real.
